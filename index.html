Scope and model limits. PMICBook v1 is an educational resource. It does not represent a foundry process recipe or replace device characterization, circuit simulation, qualification, or product-specific design. Numerical examples are cited public values or clearly labeled illustrative assumptions. BCD cross-sections are simplified 2D teaching models.
