Key Takeaways

  1. PMIC performance links power-domain needs to circuit choices, device limits and integration trade-offs.
  2. BCD combines low-voltage CMOS, bipolar functions and high-voltage devices on a technology-dependent platform.
  3. Fabrication choices shape parasitics, current handling, isolation, thermal behavior and reliability.
  4. Characterization combines electrical tests with wafer monitors and production screening under defined conditions.
  5. Models and simulators support engineering intuition; they do not replace a product specification or foundry data.
