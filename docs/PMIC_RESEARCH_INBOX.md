This inbox records useful external reports or standards whose full text or authoritative page could not be confirmed from the available evidence. Access status applies to the cited item, not to PMICBook's claims as a whole. A bibliography entry or abstract is not evidence that the full report was read. No ChatGPT Work, private library, institutional login, or user account access is assumed.

## Intake rules

- Update status only after an actual access attempt; distinguish **HTTP failure**, **paywall/login**, **metadata only**, and **full text read**.
- Capture exact title, authors, date, DOI or stable URL, page/section, access date, and the claim it supports.
- Record the narrow claim supported and what the source does not establish. Standards listings, abstracts, and vendor overviews do not substitute for normative text or platform data.
- Never place confidential workplace or foundry information here. No external service, account, or paid access was used for this inventory.
