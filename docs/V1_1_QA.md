## Validation plan and evidence

Local candidate validation completed on the project-path-mapped site (`http://127.0.0.1:18765/pmicbook/`). `tools/check_site.py` found 25 paired subject chapters and 53 routes with no broken local links/fragments or input/quiz parity failures. JavaScript syntax, Python compilation, license-comment path resolution, and `git diff --check` passed. Real Chrome loaded all 53 routes without unexpected console, network, canvas, or KaTeX errors; actual drawer, pager, TOC, English/Korean switch, and compatibility redirect destinations were verified under `/pmicbook/`. Chapter and final quiz flows, theme persistence, LDO, converter, BCD stepper, four mobile views, and source-model assertions passed.

Lab replay boundary checks ran sequentially in Chrome for English and Korean. The largest accepted single anneal cases at 1200 °C in N2 were fine 3 s, standard 14 s, and wide 108 s; one additional second was rejected in each domain. At 800 °C wet oxidation, the largest accepted cases were 89 s, 178 s, and 447 s respectively; one additional second was rejected. A 1200 °C / 3400 s anneal was rejected for N2, dry, and wet ambient in all domains; the same hostile case was rejected when repeated 16 times. A 32-step strip sequence was accepted and 33 steps rejected. All 60 accepted/rejected workload restores finished in at most 1.37 s; five malformed fragment types (invalid Base64, invalid JSON, unknown operation, unknown field, oversized fragment) in both locales displayed the localized invalid-link notice and restored the eight-step default with no runtime exception in at most 1.23 s. Hostile 100-step lithography shares fell back to the eight-step default in 1.33 s (EN) and 1.46 s (KO). All seven presets in both languages loaded and reloaded their share state or displayed the share-limit explanation (maximum observed preset load 0.90 s). The harness diagnostic check attributed its deliberately injected exception and blocked request to the correct chapter and reported no unexpected issues.

Metadata was regenerated on 2026-10-09; a second generator run produced an unchanged diff. Production Pages deployment and browser validation of the final pushed commit remain pending and are release gates. The final deployed URL, exact commit, Pages build result, and any remaining limitations must be recorded in `PRODUCTION_QA.md` after that run.

Public-source citations and assumptions are in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md) and the changed lessons. External KaTeX/fonts/selected visuals still depend on public CDNs; the models are educational and do not predict foundry or product behavior.
