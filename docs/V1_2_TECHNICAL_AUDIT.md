## Evidence versus inference versus unknown

- **Evidence:** 25 chapter IDs/routes and quiz-correct counts match between EN and KO in the bounded audit. Section IDs also match across the 25 pairs. The v1.1 record documents prior technical corrections and source-traceability work. The observed candidate diff changes the Chapter 24 lab sequence, cross-section notes, p+ body tie, and cited chronology in both languages; Chapter 18 adds a two-branch sequence discussion.
- **Inference:** The old ordered preset could be read as a representative manufacturing sequence despite its educational label. The candidate now names one source-bounded example and adds a contrasting flow; the bounded source/physics review and bilingual stage interactions checked that correction. The unresolved question is whether either disclosed sequence is prevalent beyond its cited example; the reviewed sources do not establish that.
- **Unknown:** No reviewed evidence establishes a universal nLDMOS module order or production prevalence. No private foundry flow, confidential process data, or product-specific specification was used. Native-speaker review and real process qualification remain outside this audit.
